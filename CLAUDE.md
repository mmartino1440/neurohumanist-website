## Purpose

The Neurohumanist is Michael's Substack (and GSR cross-posting platform) for long-form essays at the intersection of neuroscience, medicine, and human experience. Voice: direct, personal, intellectually curious, occasionally impassioned — not academic-passive or listicle-formatted. Audiences include fellow trainees, science-curious general readers, and the GSR readership.

## Voice Guidelines

- Personal anecdotes anchor larger arguments
- Direct, opinionated — not hedged or academic-passive
- Paragraphs, not bullet lists
- Minimal headers (one or two at most per piece, if needed)
- Connects personal → systemic → policy
- Occasional profanity is authentic, not gratuitous
- Asks real questions — doesn't always resolve them neatly
